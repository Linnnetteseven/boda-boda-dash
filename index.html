How to play

Runner

Switch lanes with ← → or A / D, hop with Space or ↑. Potholes and matatus end your ride. Passengers pay Ksh 50, coins Ksh 10.

Free ride

Drive with W A S D or the arrows. Shift boosts, Space hops. Pick up a waving passenger at a stage, then ride to the green beam to drop them off. Faster drops pay a tip.

    In Runner, pick up 3 passengers in a row without missing one and you get a free power-up.
